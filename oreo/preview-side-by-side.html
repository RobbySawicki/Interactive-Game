WHAT TO LOOK FOR

  1. Enter a name on the iPad — when you click SLING IT, the truck side panel flashes NEW HERO with your name.
  2. Drag the cookie — the iPad's power meter fills 0 → 100%. Release. The truck reacts the instant the projectile leaves the slingshot.
  3. Land a hit and watch the combo on the truck climb. Three+ in a row triggers a giant POW! burst on the side panel.
  4. Score updates live on the truck LED. The rear panel pulses with the latest hit value and shows the QR for the campaign drop.
  5. Time runs out — the truck shows your final score and name on the leaderboard rail. Spectators see the same thing the player does.
